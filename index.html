♟ How to play — Beginner Guide
  • 1. Start a game: Tap New Game, choose Vs AI or 2 Player, then start.
  • 2. Select a piece: Tap your chess piece. Legal destination squares will highlight.
  • 3. Move: Tap the highlighted square where you want the piece to go.
  • 4. Learn the goal: Checkmate the opponent's king while keeping your own king safe.
  • 5. Clock: Each turn has 15 seconds. A timeout returns that player's clock to 15s; the 5th timeout loses the game.
  • 6. Flip Board: Use Flip Board to rotate the board and change the playing orientation.
🤖 AI Modes — What is the difference?
  • Easy: Quick, beginner-friendly play. Makes simpler choices and occasional random moves.
  • Medium: More consistent tactical decisions and deeper search than Easy.
  • Hard: Stronger tactical search and more careful move selection.
  • Grandmaster: The strongest available mode in this game, using the deepest search budget. It is intended to be very challenging.
  • 2 Player: No AI difficulty is used. Both sides are human players sharing the same device.
♟ 2 Player — Pass & Play
  • Choose 2 Player when two people want to play on one phone, tablet, or PC.
  • White makes a move first, then hand the device to Player 2 for Black.
  • There is no AI. Easy / Medium / Hard / Grandmaster do not apply.
  • Both players use the same 15-second turn clock and timeout rule.

🧠 Practice Steps — Learn Chess From Zero
  • Practice 1: Learn how each piece moves — pawn, rook, knight, bishop, queen, king.
  • Practice 2: Start Easy AI games and focus on making legal moves without rushing.
  • Practice 3: Before every move, ask: “Is my king safe? What can the opponent capture?”
  • Practice 4: Try to control the center with pawns and develop knights/bishops early.
  • Practice 5: Move to Medium when Easy feels comfortable; use Hard and Grandmaster as challenge levels.
  • Practice 6: Play 2 Player with a friend and review each game by discussing why each move was chosen.
